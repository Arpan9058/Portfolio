How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 1

    Scope

    A short call to pin down what the project has to do, who it is for, and what "done" means. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything starts.

  2. 2

    Build

    Work happens in the open on GitHub, in small commits you can follow. A preview link goes up early so you are never waiting on a big reveal.

  3. 3

    Review

    You test the real thing on real devices. Revision rounds are part of the price, not an extra line item.

  4. 4

    Ship

    Automated deploys, a handover doc, and the source in your own repository. You own everything at the end.
